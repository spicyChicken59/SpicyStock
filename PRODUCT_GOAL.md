# SpicyStock product goal

Set 9 October 2026. This is the active product goal and release sequence.

Build a dependable morning workspace for an educated first real-money trade,
then make that preparation repeatable. The owner uses a **$2,000 cash account**
and **America/Chicago** time. The broker is not yet specified. The desired
personal outcome is a first profitable trade; product acceptance is based on
verifiable data, usable decisions and accurately measured outcomes, not a
promise that a particular trade will win.

A useful morning can end in a conditional trade or an explained wait. Publish
the best supported opportunities available each session; do not manufacture a
daily ticket by dropping a market, data, event or account constraint. A more
speculative idea may be useful research when its added risks, missing evidence
and experimental status are explicit.

## Operating contract

- Use the existing SpicyChicken design system and GitHub Pages deployment.
- Keep provider credentials in server-side repository secrets. The browser
  receives public research and stores optional private notes locally.
- The product does not place broker orders. Broker quotes, spread, order terms,
  available settled cash and actual fills need explicit confirmation.
- For the first release, scheduled model sizing uses $2,000 equity, 0.5% base
  risk ($10 before strategy reductions), a 25% per-name cap ($500), and the
  existing four-slot ceiling. These are model limits, not observed buying power.
  A stop price does not cap the loss from a gap or slippage.
- Cash-account preparation uses settled cash. Do not infer it from equity or
  immediately recycle expected sale proceeds. Settlement guidance must name
  its basis; the broker remains the source of the actual available amount.
- Label source, session, observation time, coverage and missing fields. Keep
  historical publications immutable, including their old account assumptions.
- Apply one cash/slot allocation across reaction and anticipation plans.
  Keep uncertain model positions occupied until their disposition is known.
- Make known corporate-action risks explicit and withhold affected momentum
  tickets. A bounded manual event list is not comprehensive news coverage.
- Save every strategy change with its rules identity. Evaluate new ideas on
  retained data before presenting their performance as live experience.

## Milestones and acceptance

### 1. Trustworthy morning preparation — accepted 9 October 2026

Deliver a compact morning desk, opened from the masthead, with Chicago session times, publication and
entry-window status, separate reaction/anticipation counts, the reason for
waiting, cash-account preparation and a visible sizing mismatch when an older
publication uses a different account. Settled cash begins unknown. Checkboxes
record preparation only; they cannot authorize a withheld setup.

Repair the first audit's concrete defects: shared allocation omits anticipation
plans; a verified takeover appears as a momentum setup; delayed scheduled jobs
can use the wrong session or repeat work; production sizing assumes $10,000.

Acceptance: pipeline and provenance agree on both types of ticket and total
commitment; cash/slots are never double-spent; known excluded events carry a
source and reason; delayed schedules cannot publish the next incomplete session
or overwrite newer evidence; phone and desktop show the same actionable state;
tests and public asset verification pass. A subsequent real publication must
demonstrate the new $2,000 sizing before this is accepted on live data.

### 2. A verified morning check

Add a secure read-only morning data refresh with explicit provider entitlements,
quote/bar times and delays. Validate price against trigger, limit and stop;
display spread and liquidity where the feed supports them. Check issuer news,
earnings, corporate actions and trading halts against named sources. Show
missing coverage as a reason to verify or wait. Do not label delayed SIP or a
single-venue quote as consolidated real-time data.

Reduce the initial publication payload as part of this milestone. The starting
JSON is about 14.79 MB before transport compression; keep the first morning
decision fast while loading detailed retained evidence on demand.

Acceptance: an on-time refresh before the normal 8:30 a.m. Chicago open, visible
staleness during the entry window, actual halt/event test cases, provider outage
behavior, no client credentials, and a reproducible decision for each retained
candidate. Confirm the broker before adding broker-specific instructions.

### 3. Better opportunity coverage

Measure the complete candidate funnel: discovery, mechanical quality, review
selection, accepted review, event checks, valid entry geometry, whole-share
size, allocation and eventual outcome. Prioritize scarce reviews for candidates
that could produce a usable plan under the current regime and account, while
preserving enough retained evidence to detect selection bias.

Study less restrictive or alternative setups as separately identified research.
Report sample size, hold period, uncertainty, drawdown and costs; compare against
the unchanged baseline on separate evaluation periods. Do not equate a visual
grade with a probability of profit or increase the daily trade count as a goal
in itself.

Acceptance: explain where each potentially usable candidate was lost, quantify
review spend and coverage, reproduce proposed improvements, and promote only
an explicitly reviewed strategy version. A small account must be tested with
whole shares and realistic entry/exit costs.

The first real-provider rehearsal confirms that review acceptance alone is not
the bottleneck. HAE's rejected reply attempted a C grade and cited paths outside
the allowed evidence contract. Even without that review refusal, its $2,000
model has only $1.25 effective risk after the existing market, burst and stop
reductions, below its $4.84 risk per share. Constrain reader citations to the
actual allowed paths and account for whole-share viability when studying review
selection; never turn invalid evidence into a trade merely to fill the list.

### 4. First-trade walkthrough and review

Provide one coherent journey from a qualified setup to broker verification,
quantity, maximum planned cost, stop, cancellation conditions and dated exit
plan. Support optional private capture of the owner's actual order/fill/exit
without treating a clicked button as execution. Keep actual results separate
from daily-bar model outcomes. A user-reported winning trade only becomes a
recorded successful trade after its actual entry, exit, quantity and costs are
provided.

Acceptance: an end-to-end rehearsal with a realistic $2,000 cash balance;
invalid or unconfirmed cash cannot produce a ready state; original evidence
remains available through the trade's life; realized P&L is reproducible from
reported executions. The owner executes the real-money trade at the broker.

### 5. Dependable daily operation

Track publication and morning-refresh success, delivery latency, provider
coverage, unresolved event checks, review acceptance, eligible/allocated
tickets and subsequent outcomes. Put a readable service state on the site.
Make recovery idempotent and keep monitoring independent of signal quality.

Acceptance: ten consecutive expected sessions with verified publication and
morning checks, a rehearsed provider/reader failure, bounded costs, and an
outcome review based on settled observations. Daily preparation is the service
commitment; daily profits and daily tickets are not.

## Starting evidence

The 8 October publication, inspected on 9 October, contains 730 discovered
reaction candidates, 12 requested chart reviews and 11 accepted reviews. It
publishes zero reaction tickets and two anticipation plans. Its $10,000 account
assumptions do not match the owner's account. The shared cash-budget block
reports no allocation even though those anticipation plans reserve model funds.
MG is among them despite a documented pending cash transaction. The publication
is degraded; the existing browser can still offer valid unaffected plans under
that state, so degraded must not be confused with universally blocked.

The repository's retained research is not evidence of a proven edge. Its
published counterfactual and historical findings remain the performance source
until new, separately identified results are produced. Closing this goal means
shipping the milestones and reviewing actual experience, not marking an
unobserved financial outcome complete.

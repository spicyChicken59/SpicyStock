a day buy stop-limit with a one-triggers-the-other sell stop

The ticket

  • Buy stop (close_plus_cushion): the burst close plus the larger of $0.02 and 0.1% of the close.
  • Limit: the close plus 4% (the outer extension line, as the record archives it), narrowed to the highest price at which the stop is still within 4% of the limit.
  • Stop: the burst day's low, else its range midpoint, the first within 4% of the limit and under the buy stop; a ticket neither can carry is withheld. A stop wider than 2% multiplies the risk by 0.5.
  • Size: shares at the limit (order_limit) risking 0.5% of the $30,000 model account times the light's multiplier, capped at 25% of it, 4 model slots. Model sizing, never the owner's holdings.

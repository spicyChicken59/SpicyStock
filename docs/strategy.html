what the scans find

Discovery

  • The 4% burst scan: the close at least 1.04× the previous close, volume above the previous session's, and at least 100,000 shares.
  • The dollar scan: the close at least $0.90 over the session's own open (the day's body, the gap excluded), on more than 100,000 shares.
  • The admission gate (burst_route_or_dollar_expansion_v1): a name the 4% scan found is admitted; a name the dollar scan alone found is admitted only on a range expansion and higher volume day (the checklist's RE and VOL checks). Otherwise: dollar scan alone, without a range-expansion, higher-volume day: research only, no ticket.

The burst ticket

  • Buy stop (close_plus_cushion, the rule this record was made under): the burst close plus the larger of $0.02 and 0.1% of the close.
  • Limit: the close plus 4% (the outer extension line), narrowed to the highest price at which the stop is still within 4% of the limit.
  • Stop: the burst day's low, else its range midpoint, the first within 4% of the limit and under the buy stop; a ticket neither can carry is withheld.
  • Size: shares at the limit (order_limit) risking 0.5% of the $30,000 model account times the light's multiplier, times 0.5 for a stop wider than 2% of the limit, and times 0.5 for either hazard: a burst day up 15% or more, or a close more than 20% over its 20-session average (the two hazards take the smaller, never their product); capped at 25% of the account, 4 model slots. Model sizing, never the owner's holdings.
  • Entry window: the first 30 minutes of the session; an unfilled order is cancelled at the window's end. Skip lines: an open more than 2% under the burst close (a burst already failing), or more than 4% over it (the follow-through spent before the fill; over 8% it is an entry the exits would be selling into).

The order the model applies them in

The model's walk (plan.follow()) reads each session in this order, and the first step that closes the position ends it:

  1. stop_at_open: an open at or under the stop: stopped at the open
  2. gap_20pct: an open at or over the gap-exit level: the whole position sold at the open
  3. stop_intraday: a low at or under the stop: stopped at the stop (the stop wins over any later level that day)
  4. sell_half_8pct: a high at or over the sell-half level, once: half sold at that level (or the open, if higher), and the stop raised under the day's high
  5. abnormal_10pct: a close at or over the abnormal-close level: the stop raised under the day's high; the model sells nothing on it
  6. day3_no_progress: on the no-progress day, a close at or under the fill: the whole position exits at the close
  7. day3_sell_half: on the sell-half day, half not yet sold: half sold at the close
  8. entry_day_low: on the entry day the stop rises to that day's low; from the trail day it rises to each day's low
  9. day5_exit: on the final day: the remainder sold at the close

Attribution. no_breakeven is a third party's measurement, theStrat Lab EP9M backtest, of a different setup (the episodic pivot on at least 9,000,000 shares), carried over by this build and not validated on bursts here.
